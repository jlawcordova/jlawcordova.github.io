# Footer

The one-line footer, drawn as a grass block: a pixel grass layer along the top over dirt, with the copyright on the left and links elsewhere on the right, all in pixel type.

## Use

- `.site-footer`: `color-on-footer` on `color-footer`, `label`-sized Silkscreen (12px, 1px tracking), 22px vertical padding plus the gutter.
- The grass block: a 28px grass edge across the top in the hero island's greens, with a light highlight row and a stepped edge that drips into the dirt. Below it, `color-footer` dirt carries sparse 4px speckles one step lighter and darker. The grass and speckles are illustration: keep them faint and behind the text, and keep the text at `color-on-footer`.
- Every link is at least 44 × 44px, even the one-letter X. On hover or focus it's underlined. Focus uses a `color-on-forest` outline over a `color-footer` halo.

## What you provide

- The year and up to four uppercase link labels.
