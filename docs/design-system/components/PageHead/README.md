# PageHead

The title band at the top of every non-home page: a `color-hero` band under the isometric grid, with an optional pixel label above the `h1`.

## Use

- `.page-head.isogrid > .page-head__inner`: 56px top and 40px bottom padding (40px and 32px at 480px and below). The inner block is `content-max` wide, or `reading-max` with `.page-head--reading` for posts and documents.
- The label is the place for type and date ("DECISION · 2026-10-04"), in `label`.

## What you provide

- The title, which says the subject plainly. Optionally, a label.

## Don't

- Don't put buttons or a lede in the band. The page body starts the content.
